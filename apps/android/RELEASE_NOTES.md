**Download MoneyLens.apk below on your Android phone, then open it to install.**

Android is careful with apps that don't come from the Play Store, so you may see one of these. They're expected, and each has a way through:

- **"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"**: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**. This is per app, so allow the one you opened the file from (Chrome, Files or WhatsApp).
- **"Blocked by Play Protect"** or **"Unsafe app blocked"**: tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. Play Protect warns about every app it hasn't seen in the Play Store.
- **Samsung phones, "Blocked by Auto Blocker"**: open **Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker**, turn it off, install MoneyLens, then turn it back on.
- **Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Vivo or Oppo phones**: the phone's own security scan may ask first; choose to continue or install anyway.
- **"App not installed"** or **"There was a problem parsing the package"**: the download was cut short or renamed. Download it again in Chrome, from this page, rather than a forwarded copy.

Your account, statements and analysis stay on your phone. The app only goes online if you turn on MoneyLens AI in Settings with your own Gemini key, and then it sends just your question and that month's totals. Installing a newer version keeps your data.
